Privacy policy

Last updated 23 February 2026

Information We Collect

We collect personal information from you, including your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Pet details (Name, breed, age, and health notes)

We collect this information when you make an online booking, sign up for our newsletter, or contact us through our website.

We also collect website usage information through analytics tools when you browse our site. This information is collected in an aggregated and anonymised form and is not used to personally identify individual visitors.

Why We Collect It

We collect personal information to process your online bookings, maintain accurate pet health records for grooming safety, and respond to your enquiries.

Website usage information is collected to understand how visitors use our site, improve website performance, measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns, and enhance the overall user experience.

Storage and Security

We keep personally identifiable information secure by storing it in encrypted digital files and restricting access to authorised staff members only.

Website usage data collected through analytics tools is stored by trusted third party providers in accordance with their security and privacy policies.

Providing some personal information is optional; however, if you choose not to enter required contact or pet details, we will be unable to provide online booking services.

Retention and Your Rights

We keep your information for two years, after which it is securely destroyed. You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you and to ask for it to be corrected.

Contact Us

For any privacy concerns, contact us at hello@snazzypawsgrooming.co.nz or (000) 123 12345.
